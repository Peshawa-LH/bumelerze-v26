About Bumelerze

Bumelerze was built by a Kurdish earthquake researcher. It takes earthquake data from USGS, EMSC and GEOFON and shows it in Sorani, Kurmanji, Arabic and English. It is free, has no ads, and does not sell data.

When the ground shakes here, people search for news in their own language and often find rumours. Bumelerze answers with named sources, in plain words, and asks what you felt so the shaking can be mapped. Earthquakes cannot be predicted. This app reports what has happened.

How to spot a fake

  1. The real app is only at bumelerze.com/app. It is not in any app store yet.
  2. Bumelerze never predicts earthquakes. Any message that predicts one is not from Bumelerze.
  3. Bumelerze is free. It never asks for money, card details or passwords.
