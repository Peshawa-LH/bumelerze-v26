Privacy policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

Bumelerze is an independent earthquake monitoring system for Kurdistan and Iraq.

What we collect

  • Felt reports, only when you choose to send one: the shaking level you pick, any optional details, an optional photo or comment, your location if you allow it, and the time.
  • Feedback, only when you choose to send it: your message, an optional contact address, optional screenshots, and the app version, language and platform.
  • Account details, only if you create an account: your email address, a password (stored only in hashed form), the name, @username and optional photo on your profile, and an optional profession that is never shown to others.
  • Comments, posts and follows, only when you write them or follow someone.
  • Homes, only if you use Tag my building: the home's exact location, your answers about the building, its photos and its family members. Only the approved members of that home can see them.
  • What you agreed to: the version and date of the terms and of the research consent you accepted; and, before your first comment or post, the version and date of the community guidelines you accepted, with your confirmation that you are 13 or older.
  • A daily activity record: once a day, when the app opens, it records the time, the platform (iOS, Android or web), the app version and the language against your account or your install's random ID. It records no location. We use it to count active users and to help when something goes wrong.
  • An anonymous launch count: when the app opens, and only if you have already allowed location, it records a coarse region (city scale) and the platform, with no identifier.

How we use it

Felt reports are combined into community shaking maps. Your individual report and your exact location are never published. Feedback is used to improve the app. If you choose the optional research consent, your reports and building information are also used for earthquake research and to improve Bumelerze's damage estimates, without your name and with the location coarsened. We do not sell or share your data, and the app contains no advertising and no third-party trackers.

Location

Location is requested only while you use the app and only with your permission. If you decline, the app still works and you can pick your town from a list instead.

Accounts

Accounts are optional. Without one, the app uses random IDs made for your install instead of your name; one of them is shown in the app as your Contributor ID. If you create an account, we keep your email address so we can help you get back into your account: if you lose your password, write to us through Feedback and an administrator can set a temporary password, which you then change. We send no emails at present.

What administrators can see

Bumelerze administrators can see a list of accounts and of installs without an account, including an install's random ID. For each they see the sign-up date, the last activity, the platform, the app version, the language, and the number of felt reports, comments, posts, homes and feedback messages. On a person's page they also see recent comments and posts, the time and shaking level of recent felt reports, any restrictions, and private notes written by administrators. A device is shown only as a short code derived from a hash, never as its full device identifier.

Moderators see accounts only, without email addresses. A smaller group of administrators can also see installs without an account and a masked email address (for example a***@example.com), and the full address after confirming. Administrators cannot see your exact location, the details of your homes or your password. Opening a person's record and revealing an email address are both logged, with who did it and when.

Moderation

Other users can report comments, posts and profiles, and moderators review them. When a moderator removes a comment or post, a private copy of its text is kept for 90 days as evidence and then deleted. Text you delete yourself is hidden at once and wiped after 30 days; you can restore it within 24 hours. An account, or an install without one, can be warned, limited or suspended for breaking the community guidelines; you can ask for a review in the app. Felt reports, alerts, feedback and deleting your account are never blocked.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app, under Profile. Deletion cannot be undone.

What is deleted: your email address and password; your profile, photo and @username; your posts, your followers and the people you follow; the homes you own, with their answers, photos and family links (family members lose access), and your membership of other homes; your alert places, ranks and badges; your daily activity record and your acceptance of the guidelines. The text of your comments is wiped; a “Deleted account” line stays in the thread so replies still make sense.

What stays: your felt reports (and photos attached to them) stay as research data, without your name; their link to your account is removed. Feedback you sent stays, without your name or contact details. Moderation records of reports and removals stay, without your name. If your account is limited or under review, copies of removed text are kept for up to 90 days.

Your data

To ask about or delete the reports tied to your install, write to dev@bumelerze.com and quote the Contributor ID shown inside the app. If you have an account, you can also delete it yourself in the app (see above).
