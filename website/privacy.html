Privacy policy

Effective date: 18 August 2026

The short version

  • Bumelerze has no accounts and no sign-up: we never ask for your name, email address, or phone number.
  • No ads, no ad SDKs, no third-party analytics or trackers.
  • We never sell or share your data for advertising or any commercial purpose.
  • When you send a felt report, it is transmitted to and stored in Bumelerze's own database so we can build community shaking maps. Your individual report is never shown to the public: only combined, aggregated data is published, once enough reports exist for an area.

What the app collects

Anonymous identity

The first time you open the app, it creates an anonymous identity for your install (using Supabase's anonymous authentication) and generates a random device identifier on your phone. Neither is linked to your name, email address, or phone number: there is no sign-up or account to create, and a fresh install always gets a fresh identity.

Felt reports

When you choose to send a “I felt it!” report, it can include: the shaking level you picked, an optional damage grade and building type, your answers to the optional follow-up questions, an optional comment, an optional photo, your location at the time of the report (see below), and a timestamp. Sending a report, and every optional field inside it, is always your choice.

Location

The app asks for location permission only while you are using it: it never requests background or “always on” location. Location is used for two things: showing the distance from you to each earthquake, and attaching a position to your felt report so it is valid scientific testimony. Location is optional: if you deny the permission, felt reports still work; you pick your town from a list instead.

Where your data goes

Felt reports you send are transmitted to and stored in Bumelerze's own database (hosted on Supabase, in the EU, Frankfurt). That data belongs to Bumelerze and is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.

  • An optional photo you attach goes into private storage that only the Bumelerze project can read. It is held for moderation and is not public.
  • An optional comment is checked by moderation before it can appear publicly.
  • Your individual felt report, including its exact location, is never shown to the public. Aggregated shaking data for an area is published only once enough reports exist there (currently at least three), so no single report can be identified on the public map.
  • Bumelerze's computed SHAKEmap products, published openly in the Bumelerze Atlas, are built from this aggregated, scientific data and contain no personal information.
  • The only other network requests the app makes are read-only requests to public earthquake data services (USGS, EMSC, GEOFON); those contain nothing about you.

Telemetry

The app sends a small, anonymous ping when it launches, at most once per app session, so we can get a rough count of active installs. It records only a coarse location (city/region scale, not your exact position) and your device's platform (iOS, Android, or web). It carries no device identifier of any kind, not even the anonymous one described above, and cannot be linked back to you or to your felt reports. This is disclosed in the app's Settings screen.

Deleting your data

Write to hello@bumelerze.com and mention your in-app device ID (shown inside the app, so you can share it without revealing anything personal) to ask about the reports tied to your device. Bumelerze does not yet have a fully automated self-service deletion tool; email is the way to reach us today.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised before the change applies.
