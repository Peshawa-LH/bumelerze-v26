Getting started

What is Bumelerze?

Bumelerze is an earthquake information app for Kurdistan and Iraq. It shows recent earthquakes from international seismic networks, lets you share what you felt, makes its own SHAKEmaps and damage estimates, and has safety guides. It works in Sorani, Kurmanji, Arabic and English. It reports earthquakes that have happened. It does not predict them.

Where do I get the app?

Open bumelerze.com/app in your phone’s browser. That is the only official address. Bumelerze is not in any app store yet, so an app with this name in a store is not official. To keep Bumelerze on your Home Screen like other apps, follow the steps for iPhone or Android below.

How do I put Bumelerze on my iPhone?
  1. Open bumelerze.com/app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap “Add to Home Screen”, then confirm. Bumelerze now opens from its own icon on your Home Screen.
How do I put Bumelerze on my Android phone?
  1. Open bumelerze.com/app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots) at the top.
  3. Tap “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”, then confirm. Bumelerze now opens from its own icon on your Home screen.
Which languages does Bumelerze use?

Sorani Kurdish, Kurmanji Kurdish, Arabic and English. You choose a language when you first open the app, and you can change it at any time in Settings, under Language. Sorani and Arabic are shown right to left.

Does it work offline or on a weak connection?

In part. Without a connection, the app shows the last earthquakes it saved and the time they were saved. A felt report, an “I’m safe” check-in or a feedback message made without signal is saved on your phone and sent when the connection returns. Maps need a connection.

Earthquake information

Where does the earthquake information come from?

Live earthquakes come from international seismic networks. Bumelerze merges their data so that each earthquake appears once, and each earthquake’s page names its source. The history of past earthquakes comes from scientific catalogues. All sources are listed on the How it works page.

Why do the magnitude or location differ from other websites?

Each seismic network calculates magnitude and location from its own stations, so the numbers can differ slightly between websites. First values are calculated fast and are often revised in the first hours as more data arrives. Each earthquake’s page shows its source and when it was last updated.

What is the difference between magnitude and intensity?

Magnitude is one number for the whole earthquake: its size at the source. Intensity is how strongly the ground shook in one place, so it is different in every town and village. The pictures you tap when you share your experience, and the colours of a SHAKEmap, show intensity.

What is a SHAKEmap?

A SHAKEmap shows how strongly the ground shook in each place around an earthquake. Bumelerze makes its own SHAKEmaps for earthquakes in Kurdistan and Iraq, from ground-motion models, nearby seismic stations and people’s reports. Open an earthquake to see its SHAKEmap when one is available. A map can take some time to appear.

What do the damage estimates mean?

Where a SHAKEmap exists, Bumelerze can combine it with data on the region’s buildings to estimate how many buildings may be damaged, and where. These are estimates, not certainties: real damage can be lower or higher. They do not replace official damage assessments. Bumelerze does not publish casualty numbers.

Can earthquakes be predicted?

No. No one can predict the day, time or place of an earthquake, and Bumelerze never does. Messages that name a day or time for an earthquake are rumours, even when someone you trust forwards them. Do not pass them on. In the app, report such posts with the reason “Fake earthquake prediction or rumour”.

Sharing your experience

How do I share my experience after an earthquake?
  1. Tap “I felt it!” on Home or on the earthquake’s page.
  2. Tap the picture that matches what you felt. Your report is sent at once.
  3. If you like, add damage pictures, answer a few questions, or add a comment or a photo. All extra steps are optional.
Do I need an account to share my experience?

No. Anyone can share what they felt without an account. If you create an account later on the same phone, your earlier reports are added to it.

Why does Bumelerze ask for my location?

Your location shows how far each earthquake is from you, and places your report where you felt the shaking, which makes the maps more accurate. It is used only while you use the app, and only if you allow it. You can say no and search for your place instead. Change this at any time in Settings.

What is stored when I share my experience, and who sees it?

Bumelerze stores the level you picked, any details, photo or comment you add, the time, and your location if you allowed it. Reports are combined by area into maps of what people felt. Your single report and your exact location are never published. A comment may appear on the earthquake’s Event hub after a moderator checks it.

Can I delete a report?

Yes. Write to dev@bumelerze.com and quote your Contributor ID. You find it in Profile, under “Privacy & data”, with a Copy button. This works with or without an account. Deleting an account does not delete felt reports: they stay as research data, without your name.

Family, “I’m safe” and your building

What is “I’m safe”?

After an earthquake in the region, “I’m safe” tells your family you are OK in one tap. It appears after you share your experience, on Home after a strong earthquake, and on your home’s Family page. Without signal, it is sent when the connection returns.

Who sees my “I’m safe” check-in?

Only approved members of your family home, and only while “Show my check-ins to this home” is on. Your location is never shared. Check-ins show for one day. Without an account or a home, nothing is stored: tap “Send a message instead” to tell your family by WhatsApp, Viber or SMS.

Can Bumelerze send help?

No. Bumelerze cannot send rescue teams or any help. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services directly. Use “I’m safe” or a message to let your family know you are OK.

How do I create a family home?

You need an account.

  1. In Profile, open “My home”, then “Tag my building”, and answer the questions.
  2. Open Family, then “Share this home”.
  3. Show the QR code or send the invite to family only. They ask to join, and you approve each request.
How do I join my family’s home?

You need an account.

  1. In Profile, open “My home”, then “Join a home”.
  2. Scan the owner’s QR code, or type the home code and secret key.
  3. Tap “Ask to join” and wait for the owner to approve.
What does Tag my building tell me?

You answer plain questions about your house or apartment. Bumelerze returns the likely building type, a vulnerability class from A (most vulnerable) to F (least vulnerable), the damage expected for buildings like yours in strong shaking, and three practical tips. It is a general guide for your type of building, not an engineer’s inspection.

Who can see my building photos and answers?

Photos are optional. Only you and the family members you approve for that home can see its photos, answers and report. Your exact location stays private. You can delete a home at any time: it stays in the trash for 14 days, so you can restore it, then it is deleted for good.

Account and the Bumelerze Network

What does an account add?

Accounts are optional. With one, your name shows on your comments, which usually appear right away. You also get a profile, posts, badges, Tag my building, a family home, “I’m safe” for your family, sign-in on other devices, and Download my data. Create one in the Profile tab with an email and a password.

Will Bumelerze send me emails?

Not at present. Bumelerze keeps your email only to help you recover your account. Because no emails are sent, the address cannot be checked, which is why you type it twice. Check it carefully.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Password reset by email is not available yet.

  1. On the sign-in screen, tap “Forgot your password?”, then “Contact us”.
  2. In the message that opens, add your username or email, and a phone number or email under Contact.
  3. You receive a temporary password. Sign in, then set a new one under Password in Profile.
What are the community guidelines?

No predictions or rumours. No private information about others. No graphic images. Be respectful. Report only what you really felt, where you really were. Comments and posts that break these rules can be removed, and accounts can be limited. Read the full rules in Settings, under Community guidelines.

How do I report a comment, post or profile?

Tap Report on the comment, post or profile, pick the closest reason, and add a note if you like. A moderator will look at it. To stop seeing someone’s comments and posts, you can also mute them. They are not told.

Why was my comment or post hidden?

Some comments wait for a moderator before they appear: comments from guests, comments with certain words, and, for a day after a strong earthquake, posts and comments from new accounts. A moderator may also hide or remove content that breaks the rules. You then see a note in your activity list, with the reason and an “Ask for review” button.

How do I download my data?

With an account, open Profile and tap “Download my data”. You get one file with what you gave Bumelerze: profile, felt reports with their places, comments, posts, homes, check-ins and more. Without an account, write to dev@bumelerze.com and quote your Contributor ID.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile and tap “Delete account” at the bottom. Before you confirm, the app lists what is deleted and what stays. Your profile, posts, the homes you own and the text of your comments are deleted. Felt reports and feedback stay, without your name. This cannot be undone.

Alerts

When will earthquake alerts come?

Alerts about recorded earthquakes are coming soon. You can already prepare in Settings, under Notifications: earthquakes near you, one more place such as where family lives, and the smallest magnitude you want to hear about.

Will Bumelerze warn me before shaking starts?

Not today. Early warning, a warning before strong shaking arrives, is a longer-term aim that Bumelerze works towards. The models are in testing. Earthquakes cannot be predicted, and alerts, when they come, will be about earthquakes that have already been recorded.

Contact

How do I report a problem or suggest an idea?

In the app, open Settings, then Feedback. Write your message, add screenshots if they help, and leave an email or phone number if you would like a reply. Without signal, the message is saved and sent later.

How can I work with Bumelerze?

Institutions, partners and contributors can write to hello@bumelerze.com.

Where do I send a data or privacy request?

Write to dev@bumelerze.com. For requests about felt reports, quote your Contributor ID from Profile, under “Privacy & data”. The privacy policy explains what Bumelerze collects and why.

Still need help?

Send feedback from Settings in the app, or write to hello@bumelerze.com. For data and privacy requests, write to dev@bumelerze.com.
