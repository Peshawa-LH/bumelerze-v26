The earthquake app built for Kurdistan

Fast, trustworthy, scientifically correct earthquake information — in your own language. Bumelerze (bûmelerze, Kurdish for “earthquake”) is built by seismic-risk researchers who work on this region every day.

  • Live earthquake feed

    Kurdistan first. Recent and historical earthquakes near you, combined from three independent scientific sources (USGS, EMSC, GEOFON) — big, clear, and readable the moment you open the app, even on a weak connection.

  • One-tap felt report

    “I felt it!” — one tap tells us what you felt, in seconds. Reports are saved even when you're offline. Every report helps build a real, community-owned picture of how earthquakes feel on the ground in Kurdistan.

  • Bumelerze SHAKEmaps

    Our own scientific shaking-intensity maps, computed for earthquakes in the region — including ones no global service ever maps. Every map shows its data sources and whether a scientist has reviewed it.

  • Safety guides

    Plain-language guidance on what to do before, during, and after an earthquake — written for Kurdistan's homes and streets, not translated from somewhere else.
