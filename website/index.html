What it does

One app, four jobs, built for how earthquakes actually happen here

  • 01

    Live earthquake feed

    Kurdistan first: earthquakes near you, merged from USGS, EMSC, and GEOFON, big and clear the moment you open the app.

  • 02

    One-tap felt report

    “I felt it!”: one tap, in seconds, even offline. Every report builds a real, community picture of how earthquakes feel on the ground.

  • 03

    Bumelerze SHAKEmaps

    Our own shaking-intensity maps, computed for earthquakes in the region, including ones no global service ever maps, with sources and review status always shown.

  • 04

    Safety guides

    Plain-language guidance for before, during, and after an earthquake, written for Kurdistan's homes and streets, not translated from elsewhere.

How it works, and how to check it

Merged data, our own SHAKEmaps, sources you can check

Every earthquake is checked against three independent networks and merged into one record. For this region, Bumelerze also computes its own SHAKEmaps, not a repost.

Three networks, one entry

USGS, EMSC, and GEOFON each report the same earthquake. Bumelerze merges them into one entry, not three conflicting ones.

Our own SHAKEmaps

For earthquakes in the region, Bumelerze computes its own EMS-98 shaking-intensity maps from ground-motion models and felt reports. Every map states its review status.
