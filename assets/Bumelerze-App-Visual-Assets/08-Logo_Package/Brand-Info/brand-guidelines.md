# Bumelerze Brand Guidelines

## Core idea

The mark joins the approved two-peak Zagros mountain profile with its original seismograph pulse. The production path begins with the complete outer silhouette extracted from `01g-signal-red`, whose waveform matches the approved family shown in `01h-burgundy-sand` and `01j-burnt-orange`. Version 1.3 replaces pixel-derived edge steps with a continuous quadratic Bézier contour while retaining the accepted ridge proportions, thickness changes, spike heights, valley depth, and endpoint spacing. The gold endpoint represents a reported observation: a signal moving from terrain to measurable community data.

## Logo versions

- **Primary horizontal:** default for websites, headers, documents, and sponsorship rows.
- **Primary stacked:** useful for square or centered compositions.
- **Symbol only:** appropriate when the name is already clearly present nearby.
- **Square app icon:** use for iOS, Android, macOS, stores, and launchers. Do not add rounded corners; operating systems add the correct mask.
- **Reversed logo:** use the ivory version on navy or another sufficiently dark background.
- **Monochrome:** use only when color reproduction is impossible.

## Clear space

Maintain clear space on every side equal to at least **one endpoint-dot diameter**. Do not allow type, edges, or other graphics inside this area.

## Minimum sizes

- Primary horizontal logo: 140 px wide digitally or 32 mm in print.
- Symbol: 32 px digitally or 8 mm in print.
- Below 32 px, use the provided favicon artwork and test on the final device.

## Wordmark

The supplied SVG wordmarks are converted to vector outlines from **Arial Rounded MT Bold**. No font installation is required to display the master SVG files. Do not retype or stretch the wordmark.

## Do not

- Change individual palette colors or introduce gradients.
- Stretch, skew, rotate, outline, or add shadows to the mark.
- Separate the mountain from the seismic pulse.
- move, recolor, or resize the gold endpoint independently.
- place the primary dark wordmark over low-contrast imagery.
- apply a manual rounded mask to store app icons.
