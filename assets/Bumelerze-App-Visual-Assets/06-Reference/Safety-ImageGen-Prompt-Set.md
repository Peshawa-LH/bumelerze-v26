# Bumelerze Safety ImageGen Prompt Set

Generation mode: built-in ImageGen, scientific-educational illustration.

This record accompanies the rendered safety masters. The supplied safety brief remains the authoritative source for composition, teaching intent, character assignment, and exclusions.

## Global visual contract

Apply this contract to every prompt:

- Square transparent RGBA illustration, at least 1024 px; final master normalized to 1254 x 1254.
- Match the established Bumelerze flat 2D Kurdish animation world: clean heavy dark outlines, rounded forms, restrained warm earthy colors, and soft flat shading.
- Preserve the assigned character's face, age, hair or head covering, skin tone, clothing type and colors, and proportions from the approved character PNG.
- One continuous RTL-neutral scene with the subject inside the central 85 percent and a silhouette readable at 120 px on light and dark backgrounds.
- No embedded text, numerals, logos, arrows, ticks, crosses, warning marks, zones, labels, borders, split panels, injuries, blood, panic, fire, smoke, flames, sparks, or explosions.
- Incorrect-action images show an ordinary uncertain action and a visible environmental hazard, with no person struck or harmed and no editorial symbol.
