Privacy policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

Bumelerze is an independent earthquake monitoring system for Kurdistan and Iraq.

What we collect

  • Felt reports, only when you choose to send one: the shaking level you pick, any optional details, an optional photo or comment, your location if you allow it, and the time.
  • Feedback, only when you choose to send it: your message, an optional contact address, optional screenshots, and the app version, language and platform.
  • An optional account: your email (to sign in and to help you back into your account; we send no emails), a hashed password, and what you choose to share, such as comments, posts, your home and “I’m safe” check-ins.
  • A daily activity record: once a day the app notes the time, platform, app version and language, with no location.
  • An anonymous launch count: when the app opens it records, if you allowed location, a coarse region (city scale) and the platform, with no identifier.

How we use it

Felt reports are combined into community shaking maps. Your individual report and your exact location are never published. Feedback is used to improve the app. We do not sell your data, and the app contains no advertising and no third-party trackers. If your profile is public, government officials approved by Bumelerze can see your name, home town, building class and latest “I’m safe”, to plan help after an earthquake.

  • Who processes it: Supabase (our database, in the EU), Cloudflare (the app), GitHub (the website and shaking maps), OpenFreeMap and MapTiler (map tiles), and the push services of Apple, Google and your browser. They see your IP address; map services also see the area you view.
  • How long we keep it: withdrawn felt reports 30 days, feedback 1 year, launch counts 13 months; guest installs with no data are removed after 90 days without use. Felt reports are kept for research; their location is stored rounded to about 100 m, and to about 1 km when an account is deleted.

Location

Location is requested only while you use the app and only with your permission. If you decline, the app still works and you can search for your place instead.

Your data

To ask about or delete the reports tied to your install, write to dev@bumelerze.com and quote the Contributor ID shown inside the app. Account holders can delete their account in the app.
